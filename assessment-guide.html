The assessment & feedback guide

How to check learning fairly and use the results to help every learner move forward.

Two purposes

Assessment for and of learning

Formative assessment happens during learning. Its job is to show what learners understand right now so teaching can adjust.

Summative assessment happens at the end of a unit or course. Its job is to measure what has been learned, often for a grade or report.

Fairness

Principles of fair assessment

Valid

It measures what it claims to measure, matched to the learning goals.

Reliable

Different markers, or the same marker on another day, would reach similar results.

Accessible

Reasonable adjustments let every learner show what they know.

Do

  • Plan the assessment when you plan the unit.
  • Use rubrics and exemplars to make standards visible.
  • Give learners time to act on feedback.

Don’t

  • Rely on a single test for important decisions.
  • Mark everything; choose what matters most.
  • Use grades alone as feedback.
