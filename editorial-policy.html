Editorial Policy

The standards behind every Pedagogy Crown guide.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Readers rely on Pedagogy Crown for clear, accurate information. These standards guide everything we publish.

Independence

We do not accept payment in exchange for coverage, and we do not promote particular courses, publishers or products. Advertisers, if present, have no influence over our content.

Research

Guides draw on established research in cognitive psychology and education, along with classroom experience. Key studies and frameworks are credited to their authors, and claims are checked against recognised sources before publication.

Responsible writing

  • We describe what research generally suggests and avoid overstating results.
  • We note where researchers disagree.
  • We explain technical terms in plain language with clear examples.
  • We use encouraging language and treat every learner with respect.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please tell us through our contact page. We review every report, correct confirmed mistakes promptly and update the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs are sourced from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and described with accurate alternative text. Interactive tools are created by us and offer general guidance only.
