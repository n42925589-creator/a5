About Pedagogy Crown

An independent educational website about the craft and science of teaching.

Our story

Big ideas, made practical

Educational theory can feel distant from a busy classroom. Pedagogy Crown connects the two, explaining major ideas about learning and assessment in plain language and turning them into tools teachers can use straight away.

We write for school teachers, tutors, trainers and anyone who helps others learn.

Practical

Ideas you can use in your next lesson.

Balanced

We present theories fairly, strengths and limits.

Inclusive

For every subject, age and setting.

Independent

No sponsored content and nothing to sell.

Our tools, the question builder, the rubric builder and the theory explorer, offer general starting points only. Read more in our Editorial Policy.
